# Hackathon Requirements

## Challenge

- BOT Chain Builder Challenge #2
- Track: AI Native Applications
- Deadline: 2026-08-22 23:59 UTC+8, or 16:59 Africa/Lagos

## Mandatory evidence

- Publicly accessible working product
- BOT Chain Mainnet contract deployment
- Real wallet connection
- Complete deposit, agent decision, execution, and withdrawal loop
- Public GitHub repository with original development history
- AI that materially drives on-chain automation

## Judging priorities

| Category | Weight | Gren evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Product completion | 30% | Complete Mainnet loop and usable dashboard |
| Mainnet integration | 25% | Verified contracts and explorer transactions |
| Innovation | 20% | Explainable AI proposals with hard policy bounds |
| User experience | 15% | Clear wallet, transaction, and decision states |
| Technical quality | 10% | Tests, role separation, documentation, and logs |

## Claim discipline

Do not use “Mainnet ready,” “verified,” “non-custodial,” or yield language until
the deployed implementation and evidence support the statement.
